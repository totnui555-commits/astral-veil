# Opening-map environment asset provenance

All four ground compositions were authored for ASTRAL VEIL with the editable deterministic drawing source in `tools/build_vertical_environments.py`. The camera, palette, material clusters, windows, parquet, astral mosaics, worn path, floor cracks, groundcover and Thornlight arena engraving are original. No commercial game textures or screenshots were sampled.

After inspecting the first running classroom capture, the flat board fills were rejected and replaced with a second original host-generated material atlas: walnut herringbone grain, irregular verdigris limestone, layered moss/loam, and fractured diagonal Thornlight stone. The unchanged master is saved outside runtime resources in `tools/art_masters/vertical_materials.png`; its exact prompt and source provenance are recorded in `tools/art_masters/VERTICAL_MATERIAL_PROVENANCE.md`. The generator normalizes the material quadrants, restrains contrast/color, mirrors tile edges and composes them into the same four 800×500 grounds. This adds no runtime atlas resource or decoded texture memory. Authored route, floor seals, north wall, window beams and prop/collision coordinates remain intact; baked shadow masks retain the material grain beneath them.

`prop_atlas.png` is an original host-native image generation result, generated on 2026-10-11. Original retained at `/workspace/generated_images/exec-e629f005-6129-4edc-811c-90c0c1f3f4d4.png`. Source is 1254×1254 RGBA, unchanged after copying. The requested 1024×1024 exact grid was not produced; the actual silhouettes were inspected and individual regions/foot anchors recorded in `manifest.json`. Godot `AtlasTexture` references those regions directly; no destructive crop, image edit or third-party asset pack was used. Every map-specific prop placement is editable in the generator, and geometry stays in the original `game/data/maps.json`.
