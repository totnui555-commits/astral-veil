Original project artwork and audio:
- Retained pixel artwork and synthesized audio are built by tools/generate_art.py.
- The opening visual slice uses original character turnarounds, illustrated
  portraits, sanctuary title imagery and a prop atlas generated for ASTRAL VEIL
  with OpenAI's native ImageGen. Generation prompts, original masters and
  normalization records are preserved under asset_sources/vertical/ where
  applicable; the prop atlas's exact provenance is recorded in
  game/art/vertical/environment/PROVENANCE.md.
- tools/build_vertical_sprites.py normalizes the original turnarounds with hard
  alpha, nearest sampling and shared palettes, then articulates pixel cutout rigs
  into the committed directional animation atlases. These animation frames are
  generated from articulated rigs rather than individually hand-drawn frames.
- tools/build_enemy_packages.py normalizes the independently generated Archer,
  Brute and Mage views and applies the same rig technique. Their exact source
  and prompt remain in asset_sources/vertical/enemies/.
- tools/build_vertical_interactables.py packs original ImageGen chest/fire/rune/
  gate artwork at actual gameplay scale. The unmodified master and generation
  provenance remain in asset_sources/vertical/interactables/.
- The original generated herringbone/limestone/moss/thornstone material source
  and provenance remain in tools/art_masters/; composition/normalization uses
  those materials in the existing four grounds without a new runtime atlas.
- tools/build_vertical_environments.py authors ground compositions and editable
  prop placement; tools/build_vertical_vfx.py authors pixel combat effect cels;
  tools/build_fantasy_ui.py authors the engraved interface materials and emblems.

The source distribution includes the editable asset_sources masters/provenance
and these builders. Runtime Web/native packs include game/ assets only, with
generation masters and QA inspection sheets kept outside the runtime tree.
No copied commercial game art, third-party asset pack or soundtrack recording is
used. The font and Godot licenses above remain applicable to those components.
